How it works & where the data comes from

The idea. A fire is the effect. Before it, clues appear: D the ocean (El Niño / Indian Ocean Dipole) shifts, A the rain stops, B the soil and peat dry out, on land already weakened by C forest loss. Click a place and see whether those clues appeared before every big fire year, and whether they are lit again now.

Every number on this map is measured. Each 0.5° cell carries monthly data from Jan 2003 to Oct 2026. The fire archive (NASA FIRMS) is published through Dec 2025; 2026 fire months are marked "not yet archived". Tree-cover loss statistics run through 2020.
