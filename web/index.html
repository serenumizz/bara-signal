Findings · the two questions

Are fire and haze really going down?

How have satellite-detected fire hotspots, aerosol optical depth at 550 nm (AOD) and surface PM₂.₅ changed across Sumatra and Kalimantan from 2003 to 2025, and are the trends statistically significant? And do the seasons with more fire hotspots coincide with higher AOD and PM₂.₅?

AOD · aerosol optical depth, 550 nmA column measure: how much sunlight all particles between the ground and space block. Unitless; includes smoke high in the air and does not say what people breathe.
PM₂.₅ · surface fine particlesA near-ground measure: mass of particles under 2.5 µm in the air at the surface, in µg/m³. Here a MERRA-2 model estimate (dust + sea salt + black carbon + organic carbon + sulfate), not a ground monitor.

Drill down: fire in one regency (Cloudflare D1 database)

Ask the data: is fire in a regency going up or down?

Pick a regency and a period. The trend (Mann–Kendall test and Sen's slope) is computed on the fly from fire counts stored in a Cloudflare D1 database, then explained in plain words.
